Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 July 2026
This policy explains what information Ghaya collects, how we use and protect it, and your choices. It applies to our website and HR platform.

1. Who controls the data

For the companies that subscribe to Ghaya, the company is the "controller" of its employees' data — it decides what is stored. Ghaya is the "processor": we store and process that data only to provide the service to that company, following its instructions.

2. Information we collect

  • Account & company details: company name, admin name, email, phone/WhatsApp, and login credentials.
  • Employee records (entered by the company): names, Civil ID, nationality, job and department, salary and allowances, leave, attendance, documents, and similar HR data.
  • Attendance location: if geofencing is enabled by the company, we process device location at clock-in/out solely to verify the employee is within the workplace area. We do not track location continuously.
  • Usage & technical data: basic logs such as IP address, device/browser, and actions taken, used for security and to improve the service.
  • Demo/trial enquiries: details you submit through our website forms.

3. How we use it

  • To provide, operate, and support the HR platform.
  • To process payroll, leave, attendance, documents, and notifications you request.
  • To send service messages, invoices, and support responses.
  • To secure the service, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use employees' personal data for advertising.

4. Notifications

If you enable push notifications, we use a third-party provider (OneSignal) to deliver them to your device. Only the information needed to send a notification is shared. You can turn notifications off at any time in your browser or device settings.

5. Where data is stored & security

Ghaya runs on trusted global cloud infrastructure (Cloudflare). We use encryption in transit, access controls, and per-company data isolation. While we take strong measures to protect data, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

6. Sharing

We only share data with: service providers who help us run Ghaya (e.g., hosting and notification delivery) under confidentiality obligations; and authorities where required by Kuwaiti law. We never sell personal data.

7. Retention

We keep company and employee data for as long as the account is active. After cancellation or non-payment closure, data is retained for a limited period (see our Terms of Service) and then deleted. You may request an export of your data before deletion.

8. Your choices & rights

  • Company admins can view, edit, or delete employee records within the platform.
  • Employees should direct requests about their own data to their employer (the controller), who can act within Ghaya.
  • You may contact us to ask about the data we hold or to request deletion, subject to legal and contractual limits.

9. Cookies

We use only essential cookies/local storage needed to keep you logged in and remember basic preferences (like language). We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

10. Children

Ghaya is a workplace tool intended for use by businesses and their adult employees. It is not directed at children.

11. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified by email or in the platform.

12. Contact

Questions about privacy or your data? Contact us at info@ghaya.com or via WhatsApp.
